This document lists the goals of astroknights

# Primary goals
- Create high quality DSO images
- Learn about astronomy and astrophotography

# Secondary goals
- Take pictures of comets and other SSO events such as solar eclipse
- Participate in citizen science such as AAVSO, Globe at night
- Nice SSO compositions
- Learn about new nice places for camping

# Nice to have
- ISS transits
- Meet new people

# Lower priority
- Planets
- Solar details (CME etc)
